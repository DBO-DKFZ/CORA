# Plaque psoriasis — demo guideline (SYNTHETIC)

This document is synthetic text written solely to exercise the CORA pipeline in the

## Severity assessment

Disease is considered **moderate to severe** in this demo document when the affected
body surface area exceeds 10%, or when the Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI)
exceeds 10, or when disease at a visible or functionally critical site causes a
Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI) above 10.

## Topical therapy

Mild plaque psoriasis is managed topically. The preferred option is a **fixed-dose
combination of calcipotriol and betamethasone dipropionate** once daily for up to
four weeks, followed by intermittent maintenance.

## Conventional systemic therapy

For moderate to severe plaque psoriasis in an adult without contraindications, the
recommended first-line conventional systemic agent in this demo document is
**methotrexate**, given once weekly with folic acid supplementation. Baseline and
periodic monitoring of full blood count, liver enzymes, and renal function is
required, and pregnancy is a contraindication.

Alternative conventional systemic agents are ciclosporin, for short-term control of
unstable disease, and acitretin, which is preferred in pustular variants.

## Biologic therapy

Biologic therapy is indicated when a conventional systemic agent has failed, is
contraindicated, or is not tolerated. Classes include tumour necrosis factor
inhibitors, interleukin-17 inhibitors, and interleukin-23 inhibitors. Latent
tuberculosis screening is required before starting a tumour necrosis factor
inhibitor.

## Comorbidity screening

All patients are screened for psoriatic arthritis at diagnosis and annually
thereafter, and for cardiovascular and metabolic comorbidity.
