A <strong>patient</strong> has <strong>multiple, pearly papules</strong> on the <strong>face</strong>. <strong>Biopsy</strong> shows a <strong>malignant tumor</strong>. Which of the following features would most likely be seen on microscopic examination?

A. Cytoplasmic viral inclusions
B. Keratin
C. Melanin
D. Palisading nuclei

LLM answer: D. Palisading nuclei
