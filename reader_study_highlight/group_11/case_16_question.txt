A <strong>24-year-old male</strong> presented with the following lesion on his <strong>genitalia</strong>, following <strong>intercourse with multiple sexual partners on multiple occasions</strong>. Which <strong>human papilloma virus</strong> is the causative agent?

A. HPV 6, 11
B. HPV 16, 18
C. HPV 31, 33
D. HPV 35, 45

LLM answer: A. HPV 6, 11
