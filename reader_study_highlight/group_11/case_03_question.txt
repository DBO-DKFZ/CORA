A child with <strong>itchy skin lesions</strong> with <strong>extensor distribution</strong> on skin and <strong>elbows</strong>, whose mother gives a history of <strong>bronchial asthma</strong>. What is the diagnosis?

A. Atopic dermatitis
B. Air borne contact dermatitis
C. Scabies
D. Allergic contact dermatitis

LLM answer: A. Atopic dermatitis
