A <strong>60-year-old</strong> <strong>farmer</strong> presents with <strong>multiple patches of discoloration</strong> on his <strong>face</strong>. Biopsy of <strong>lesional skin</strong> reveals <strong>actinic keratosis</strong>. Which of the following terms best describes this response of the skin to <strong>chronic sunlight exposure</strong>?

A. Atrophy
B. Dysplasia
C. Hyperplasia
D. Hypertrophy

LLM answer: B. Dysplasia
