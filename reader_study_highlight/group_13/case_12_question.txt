<strong>Child</strong> presents with <strong>linear verrucous plaques</strong> on the <strong>trunk</strong> with <strong>vacuolisation of keratinocytes</strong> in <strong>S.Spinosum</strong> and <strong>S.Granulosum</strong>. Diagnosis is?

A. Incontinentia pigmenti
B. Delayed hypersensitivity reaction
C. Verrucous epidermal nevus
D. Linear Darier's disease

LLM answer: D. Linear Darier's disease
