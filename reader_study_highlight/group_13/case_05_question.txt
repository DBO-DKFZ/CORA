In an <strong>8-day-old child</strong> with <strong>no history of consanguinity</strong> in the parents, the mother reports <strong>blisters</strong> and <strong>bleeding off the skin</strong> at the site of <strong>handling and pressure</strong>. There was a <strong>similar history in the previous child</strong>, which <strong>proved to be fatal</strong>. The diagnosis is –

A. Bullous pemphigoid
B. Congenital syphilis
C. Congenital epidermolysis bullosa
D. Letterer-Siwe disease

LLM answer: C. Congenital epidermolysis bullosa
