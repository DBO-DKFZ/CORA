A <strong>26-year-old female</strong> patient presented with <strong>oral ulcers</strong>, <strong>sensitivity to light</strong> and <strong>rash over the malar area of the face sparing the nasolabial folds of both sides</strong>. Which of the following is the associated condition?

A. SLE
B. Psoriasis
C. Dermatitis
D. Sturge-Weber syndrome

LLM answer: A. SLE
