He has not experienced anything like this before. He is currently <strong>sexually active with multiple partners</strong> and <strong>uses condoms inconsistently</strong>. His temperature is <strong>37.0°C (98.7°F)</strong>, pulse is <strong>67/min</strong>, respirations are <strong>17/min</strong>, and blood pressure is <strong>110/70 mm Hg</strong>. Genitourinary examination shows a <strong>shallow, nontender, firm ulcer with a smooth base</strong> along the <strong>shaft of the penis</strong>. There is <strong>nontender inguinal adenopathy bilaterally</strong>. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step to <strong>confirm the diagnosis</strong>?

A. Swab culture
B. Urine polymerase chain reaction
C. Rapid plasma reagin
D. Fluorescent treponemal antibody absorption test

LLM answer: E. Dark-field microscopy
