A <strong>35-year-old woman</strong> with <strong>no significant past medical, past surgical, family or social history</strong> presents to clinic with a <strong>recently identified area of flat, intact, pigmented skin</strong>. The patient believes that this is a <strong>large freckle</strong>, and she states that it <strong>becomes darker during the summer</strong> when she is outdoors. On physical examination, you measure the lesion to be <strong>6 mm in diameter</strong>. Which of the following is the best descriptor of this patient’s skin finding?

A. Papule
B. Macule
C. Plaque
D. Wheal
E. Ulcer

LLM answer: B. Macule
