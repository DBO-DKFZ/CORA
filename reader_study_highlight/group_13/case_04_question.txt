A <strong>15-year-old</strong> girl comes to the physician for a <strong>follow-up evaluation</strong>. She has multiple <strong>erythematous pustules and nodules over her face</strong>, for which she has received <strong>erythromycin</strong> and <strong>topical benzoyl peroxide</strong>. She is concerned that the therapy is <strong>ineffective</strong>. The physician recommends a drug on the condition that the patient agrees to use <strong>oral contraceptives</strong>. The molecular structure of the drug most likely recommended by the physician closely resembles a drug used to treat which of the following conditions?

A. EGFR-positive non-small cell lung cancer
B. Acute promyelocytic leukemia
C. BRAF-positive metastatic melanoma
D. HER2-positive gastric cancer
E. Choriocarcinoma

LLM answer: B. Acute promyelocytic leukemia
