A very ill <strong>3-year-old child</strong> is brought into the <strong>emergency room</strong> with a <strong>fever</strong>. On physical examination, the child has <strong>large cervical lymph nodes</strong> and a <strong>desquamating skin rash</strong> that involves the <strong>palms, soles, and mouth</strong>. This child should be monitored for the development of which of the following conditions?

A. Abdominal aortic aneurysm
B. Aneurysm of the aortic root
C. Berry aneurysm
D. Coronary artery aneurysm

LLM answer: D. Coronary artery aneurysm
