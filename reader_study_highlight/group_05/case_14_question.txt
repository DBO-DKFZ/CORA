A <strong>58-year-old</strong> <strong>lifeguard</strong> develops <strong>squamous cell carcinoma</strong> of the <strong>skin</strong> on his <strong>forehead</strong>. Which of the following most likely preceded the development of this carcinoma?

A. Dry, scaly, hyperkeratotic papule
B. A single, large pink patch
C. Dermatophyte infection
D. UVC exposure

LLM answer: A. Dry, scaly, hyperkeratotic papule
