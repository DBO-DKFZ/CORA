A <strong>3-month-old male infant</strong> developed <strong>otitis media</strong> for which he was given a course of <strong>Co-trimoxazole</strong>. A few days later, he developed <strong>extensive peeling of the skin</strong>; there were <strong>no mucosal lesions</strong> and the baby was <strong>not toxic</strong>. The most likely diagnosis is:

A. Toxic epidermal necrolysis
B. Staphylococcal scalded skin syndrome
C. Stevens-Johnson syndrome
D. Infantile pemphigus

LLM answer: B. Staphylococcal scalded skin syndrome
