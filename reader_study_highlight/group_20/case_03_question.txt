A <strong>12-year-old boy</strong> who spent his holiday at <strong>Kovalam beach</strong> develops <strong>pruritic haemorrhagic vesicles</strong> on his <strong>cheeks, ears, nose, and hands</strong> after <strong>12 hours</strong>. A <strong>week later</strong> the lesions <strong>crusted and healed with permanent scars</strong>. What is the most probable diagnosis?

A. Polymorphic light eruption
B. Hydroa vacciniforme
C. Actinic prurigo
D. Persistent light reaction

LLM answer: B. Hydroa vacciniforme
