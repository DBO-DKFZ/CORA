A patient with <strong>breast carcinoma</strong> was given <strong>3 months of radiation therapy</strong>. After that she was started with <strong>chemotherapy</strong>. Within a few days she developed <strong>rashes</strong> over the areas where <strong>radiation</strong> was given. Which of the following drugs can cause this condition?

A. Actinomycin
B. Bleomycin
C. Mitomycin
D. Busulfan

LLM answer: A. Actinomycin
