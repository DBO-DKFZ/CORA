A child develops <strong>blisters</strong> on exposure to <strong>sunlight</strong>. <strong>Irregular dark spots</strong> on the <strong>skin</strong> are also found. He is very likely to have a defect in which of the following mechanisms?

A. Nucleotide excision repair
B. Mismatch repair
C. Recombination repair
D. Thymine dimers

LLM answer: A. Nucleotide excision repair
