A <strong>female patient</strong> presented with complaints of <strong>hair fall</strong>. Her <strong>delivery</strong> was <strong>2 months ago</strong>. The physician diagnosed her condition as <strong>Telogen Effluvium</strong>. All of the following are true regarding <strong>telogen effluvium</strong>, EXCEPT:

A. Scarring
B. Metabolic stress
C. Recovery within 6 months
D. Insidious onset

LLM answer: A. Scarring
