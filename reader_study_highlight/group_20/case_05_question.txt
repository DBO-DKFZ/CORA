A <strong>15-year-old girl</strong> comes to the physician because of a <strong>2-week history</strong> of <strong>genital lesions</strong>. She has no history of serious illness. She is <strong>sexually active</strong> with <strong>2 male partners</strong> and uses <strong>condoms inconsistently</strong>. <strong>Vital signs are within normal limits</strong>. <strong>Pelvic examination</strong> shows <strong>multiple hyperkeratotic exophytic papules</strong> over the <strong>vulva</strong>. The lesions <strong>turn white on application of 3% acetic acid</strong>. Which of the following is most likely to have prevented this patient's condition?

A. Condoms
B. Anti-HIV prophylaxis
C. Spermicidal foam
D. Vaccination

LLM answer: D. Vaccination
