A child presents with <strong>linear verrucous plaques</strong> on the <strong>trunk</strong> with <strong>vacuolization of keratinocytes</strong> in <strong>S. Spinosum</strong> and <strong>S. Granulosum</strong>. Diagnosis is?

A. Incontinentia pigmenti
B. Delayed hypersensitivity reaction
C. Verrucous epidermal nevus
D. Linear Darier's disease

LLM answer: A. Incontinentia pigmenti
