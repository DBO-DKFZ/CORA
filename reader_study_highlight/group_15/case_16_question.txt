A <strong>45-year-old woman</strong> presents with a <strong>weeping eczematoid lesion</strong> of her <strong>nipple</strong>. Which of the following statements is false concerning her <strong>diagnosis</strong> and <strong>management</strong>?

A. Treatment is with warm compresses and oral antibiotics
B. Biopsy of the nipple, revealing malignant cells within the milk ducts, is invariably associated with an underlying invasive carcinoma
C. The appropriate treatment is mastectomy
D. The lesion always represents a high-risk disease with a significant risk of subsequent metastatic disease

LLM answer: B. Biopsy of the nipple, revealing malignant cells within the milk ducts, is invariably associated with an underlying invasive carcinoma
