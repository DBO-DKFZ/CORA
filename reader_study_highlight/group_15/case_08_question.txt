A patient presents with <strong>thrombocytopenia</strong>, <strong>eczema</strong> and <strong>recurrent infections</strong>. What is the most probable diagnosis?

A. Wiskott Aldrich syndrome
B. Agammaglobulinemia
C. Chediak-Higashi syndrome
D. Lazy leukocyte syndrome

LLM answer: A. Wiskott Aldrich syndrome
