A <strong>15-year-old boy</strong> is undergoing the bodily changes associated with <strong>puberty</strong>. He is concerned that he easily develops a <strong>foul skin odor</strong>, even with <strong>mild exercise</strong>. Which of the following <strong>glandular structures</strong> is the causative agent for this <strong>foul skin odor</strong>?

A. Mucous gland
B. Apocrine gland
C. Sebaceous gland
D. Serous gland

LLM answer: B. Apocrine gland
