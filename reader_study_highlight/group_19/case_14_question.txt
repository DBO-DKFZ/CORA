A <strong>57-year-old</strong> woman comes to the physician because of <strong>increasing wrinkles</strong> on her <strong>face</strong> and <strong>sagging skin</strong>. She says that her skin used to be <strong>smooth</strong> and <strong>firm</strong>. Examination shows <strong>diffuse xerosis</strong> and <strong>mild atrophy</strong>, <strong>laxity</strong>, and <strong>fine wrinkles</strong> on the <strong>periorbital skin</strong>. Which of the following processes is most likely involved in the development of this patient's <strong>skin findings</strong>?

A. Decrease in lysyl oxidase activity
B. Increase in collagen biosynthesis
C. Decrease in elastin fiber assembly
D. Increase in fibroblast activity
E. Increase in lipofuscin deposition
F. Decreased crosslinking of collagen fibrils

LLM answer: C. Decrease in elastin fiber assembly
