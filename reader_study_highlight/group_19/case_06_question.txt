A <strong>30-year-old female</strong> presents to the <strong>dermatology OPD</strong> with complaints of <strong>asymptomatic yellowish papules</strong> around the <strong>vermilion border</strong> of the <strong>upper lip</strong>. What is the cause of the above condition?

A. Ectopic apocrine glands
B. Ectopic sebaceous glands
C. Apocrine gland hyperplasia
D. Sebaceous gland hyperplasia

LLM answer: B. Ectopic sebaceous glands
