A <strong>45-year-old</strong> <strong>farmer</strong> has <strong>itchy</strong> <strong>erythematous papular lesions</strong> on the <strong>face</strong>, <strong>neck</strong>, <strong>'V' area of the chest</strong>, <strong>dorsum of hands</strong> and <strong>forearms</strong> for <strong>3 years</strong>. The lesions are <strong>more severe in summers</strong> and <strong>improve by 75% in winters</strong>. The most appropriate test to diagnose the condition would be –

A. Skin biopsy
B. Estimation of IgE levels in blood
C. Patch test
D. Intradermal prick test

LLM answer: A. Skin biopsy
