A <strong>girl child</strong> with <strong>verrucous lesions</strong> at <strong>2 weeks of age</strong> later developed <strong>linear bands of hyperkeratotic papules and nodules</strong> followed by <strong>whorled pigmentation</strong>. Her mother had a history of <strong>in utero fetal death</strong> and <strong>hypopigmented atrophic linear lesions</strong>. The diagnosis is

A. Neurofibromatosis
B. Xeroderma pigmentosum
C. Tuberous sclerosis
D. Incontinentia pigmenti

LLM answer: D. Incontinentia pigmenti
