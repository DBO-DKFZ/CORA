<strong>Child</strong> presents with <strong>linear verrucous plaques</strong> on the <strong>trunk</strong> with <strong>vacuolization of keratinocytes</strong> in <strong>S. spinosum</strong> and <strong>S. granulosum</strong>. Diagnosis is?

A. Incontinentia pigmenti
B. Delayed hypersensitivity reaction
C. Verrucous epidermal nevus
