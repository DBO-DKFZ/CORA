A <strong>27-year-old man</strong> on his trip to <strong>Hawaii</strong> consumes an <strong>exotic food</strong> for the <strong>first time</strong>. Just after consuming it, he developed <strong>intense pruritus</strong>, <strong>swelling of the face and lips</strong>, <strong>shortness of breath</strong>, and <strong>vomiting</strong>. What is the most likely diagnosis?

A. Steakhouse Syndrome
B. Angioneurotic edema
C. Anaphylaxis
D. Myocardial infarction

LLM answer: C. Anaphylaxis
