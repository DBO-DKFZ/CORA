A <strong>5-month-old</strong> boy presented with <strong>spontaneous nose bleeds</strong> and <strong>bloody diarrhea</strong>. There is also a history of <strong>recurrent chest and ear infections</strong> for which he was frequently hospitalized. On examination, it was found that he was suffering from <strong>atopic dermatitis</strong> and had <strong>petechiae</strong> all over the body. Lab findings suggested <strong>abnormally low platelet counts</strong> along with <strong>eosinophilia</strong> and the <strong>peripheral blood smear</strong> is given below. All of the following statements are true about the above condition except:

A. There is progressive loss of T lymphocytes in the peripheral blood and in the T-cell zones (paracortical areas) of the lymph nodes
B. Low IgM and IgG levels
C. High levels of IgA
D. Patients are prone to developing B-cell lymphomas

LLM answer: C. High levels of IgA
