A <strong>28-year-old</strong> patient presents to a medical office for a consultation regarding a <strong>mole</strong> on her <strong>nose</strong> that is <strong>increasing in size</strong>. She also complains of <strong>frequent headaches</strong>, which she associates with <strong>stress</strong> on the job. She works as a <strong>civil engineer</strong> and spends much of her time <strong>outside</strong>. Her past medical history is positive for <strong>bronchial asthma</strong>; nevertheless, her <strong>vitals are stable</strong>. The mole is <strong>8 mm in diameter</strong>, has <strong>irregular borders</strong>, and is <strong>brown in color</strong>. A <strong>biopsy</strong> is performed and sent for <strong>genetic analysis</strong>. A <strong>mutation</strong> is found. A. c-MYC
B. APC
C. BRAF
D. BCL-2
