A child develops <strong>non-blanching macules and papules</strong> on the <strong>lower extremities</strong>, <strong>mild abdominal pain</strong>, and <strong>skin biopsy showed IgA deposition</strong>. The most appropriate diagnosis is:

A. Drug induced vasculitis
B. HSP
C. Wegener's granulomatosis
D. Kawasaki disease

LLM answer: B. HSP
