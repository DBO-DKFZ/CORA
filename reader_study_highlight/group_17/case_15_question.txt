A <strong>middle-aged woman</strong> has <strong>right leg and foot nonpitting edema</strong> associated with <strong>dermatitis</strong> and <strong>hyperpigmentation</strong>. The diagnosis of <strong>chronic venous insufficiency</strong> is made. What is the treatment of choice?

A. Vein stripping
B. Pressure-gradient stockings
C. Skin grafting
D. Perforator vein ligation

LLM answer: B. Pressure-gradient stockings
