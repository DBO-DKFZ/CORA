A <strong>40-year-old man</strong> presents with a <strong>single erythematous plaque</strong> on the <strong>penis</strong>. Histologic examination reveals <strong>dysplastic cells throughout the epidermis</strong>. All of the following are true about the <strong>disease</strong> the patient is suffering from, except:

A. Intact basement membrane
B. Occurs in older uncircumcised males
C. No transformation to invasive carcinoma
D. Strong association with HPV types 16 and 18

LLM answer: C. No transformation to invasive carcinoma
