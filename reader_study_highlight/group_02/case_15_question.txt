A <strong>19-year-old</strong> <strong>Caucasian male</strong> presents to your office with <strong>hypopigmented skin</strong>. He undergoes a <strong>skin biopsy</strong> and is found to have an <strong>absence of melanocytes in the epidermis</strong>. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

A. Tinea versicolor
B. Albinism
C. Vitiligo
D. Solar lentigo
E. Melanoma

LLM answer: C. Vitiligo
