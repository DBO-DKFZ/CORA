A <strong>2-year-old</strong> born out of <strong>consanguineous marriage</strong>, had a history of the <strong>collodion membrane</strong>. He had <strong>large, thick, plate-like brown scaling</strong> with <strong>generalized distribution</strong>; <strong>no erythroderma</strong> and <strong>ectropion</strong>. Which disease is it?

A. Ichthyosis vulgaris
B. Lamellar ichthyosis
C. X linked ichthyosis
D. Netherton syndrome

LLM answer: B. Lamellar ichthyosis
