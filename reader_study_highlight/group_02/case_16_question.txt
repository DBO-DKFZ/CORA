A patient presented with <strong>scarring alopecia</strong>, <strong>thinned nails</strong>, <strong>hypopigmented macular lesions</strong> over the <strong>trunk</strong> and <strong>oral mucosa</strong>. The diagnosis is –

A. Psoriasis
B. Leprosy
C. Lichen planus
D. None

LLM answer: C. Lichen planus
