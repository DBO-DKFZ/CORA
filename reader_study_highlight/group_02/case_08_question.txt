A young man presents to the emergency department with a <strong>maculopapular rash</strong> <strong>2 weeks after healing</strong> of a <strong>painless genital ulcer</strong>. The most likely etiological agent is:

A. Treponema pallidum
B. Treponema peunae
C. Chlamydia trachomatis
D. Calymmatobacter granulomatis

LLM answer: A. Treponema pallidum
