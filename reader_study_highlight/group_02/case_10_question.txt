An <strong>8-month-old child</strong> presented with <strong>itchy, exudative lesions</strong> on the <strong>face, palms and soles</strong>. The <strong>siblings also have similar complaints</strong>. The <strong>treatment of choice</strong> in such a patient is –

A. Systemic ampicillin
B. Topical betamethasone
C. Systemic prednisolone
D. Topical permethrin

LLM answer: D. Topical permethrin
