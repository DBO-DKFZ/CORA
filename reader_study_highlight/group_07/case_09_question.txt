A <strong>43-year-old</strong> woman presents to your clinic for the evaluation of an <strong>abnormal skin lesion</strong> on her <strong>forearm</strong>. The patient is worried because her mother passed away from <strong>melanoma</strong>. You believe that the lesion warrants <strong>biopsy</strong> for further evaluation for possible <strong>melanoma</strong>. Your patient is concerned about her risk for <strong>malignant disease</strong>. What is the most important <strong>prognostic factor</strong> of <strong>melanoma</strong>?

A. Diameter > 6 mm
B. S-100 tumor marker present
C. Evolution of lesion over time
D. Genetic factors
E. Age at presentation
F. Depth of invasion of atypical cells
G. Level of irregularity of the borders

LLM answer: F. Depth of invasion of atypical cells
