A child with <strong>fever</strong> presents with multiple <strong>tender erythematous skin lesions</strong>. On microscopic examination the skin lesions are seen to have <strong>neutrophilic infiltration in the dermis</strong>. What is the diagnosis?

A. Sweet syndrome
B. Behçet's syndrome
C. Pyoderma gangrenosum
D. Juvenile dermatosis

LLM answer: A. Sweet syndrome
