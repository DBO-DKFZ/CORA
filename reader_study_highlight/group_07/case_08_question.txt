A <strong>56-year-old woman</strong> comes to the physician because of a <strong>3-month history</strong> of <strong>progressive weakness</strong>. She has no history of serious illness and takes no medications. Her vital signs are within normal limits. Physical examination shows a <strong>violaceous rash over her eyelids</strong> and <strong>flat-topped erythematous papules over the dorsal surface of interphalangeal joints</strong>. <strong>Muscle strength is 4/5 at the shoulders and hips</strong> but normal elsewhere. This patient is at greatest risk for which of the following conditions?

A. Pheochromocytoma
B. Hodgkin lymphoma
C. Renal clear cell carcinoma
D. Oat cell lung cancer
E. Ovarian adenocarcinoma

LLM answer: E. Ovarian adenocarcinoma
