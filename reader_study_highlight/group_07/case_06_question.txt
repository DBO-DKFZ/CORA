An <strong>excisional biopsy</strong> is performed and the diagnosis of <strong>superficial spreading melanoma</strong> is confirmed. The lesion is <strong>1.1 mm thick</strong>. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?

A. Surgical excision with 0.5-1 cm safety margins only
B. Sentinel lymph node study only
C. Surgical excision with 1-2 cm safety margins only
D. Surgical excision with 1-2 cm safety margins and sentinel lymph node study
E. Surgical excision with 0.5-1 cm safety margins and sentinel lymph node study
F. Surgical excision with 1 cm safety margins only

LLM answer: D. Surgical excision with 1-2 cm safety margins and sentinel lymph node study
