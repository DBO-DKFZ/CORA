A <strong>17-year-old</strong> <strong>Latin American woman</strong> with <strong>no significant past medical history</strong> or <strong>family history</strong> presents to her <strong>pediatrician</strong> with concerns about several <strong>long-standing skin lesions</strong>. She notes that she has had a <strong>light-colored rash</strong> on her <strong>chest</strong> and <strong>abdomen</strong> that has been present for the last <strong>2 years</strong>. The <strong>blood pressure</strong> is <strong>111/81 mm Hg</strong>, <strong>pulse</strong> is <strong>82/min</strong>, <strong>respiratory rate</strong> is <strong>16/min</strong>, and <strong>temperature</strong> is <strong>37.3°C (99.1°F)</strong>. LLM answer: A. Malassezia yeast
