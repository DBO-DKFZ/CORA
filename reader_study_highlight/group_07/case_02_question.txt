A lady comes with <strong>melanotic pigmentation of the lip</strong>, presence of <strong>multiple polyps in the intestine</strong>, and a <strong>positive family history</strong>. What is the most probable diagnosis?

A. Peutz-Jeghers Syndrome
B. Gardner's syndrome
C. Turcot's syndrome
D. Lynch syndrome

LLM answer: A. Peutz-Jeghers Syndrome
