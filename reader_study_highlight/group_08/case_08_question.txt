A <strong>25-year-old female</strong> comes to the <strong>OPD</strong> with a history of <strong>spots and red bumps</strong> that are located principally on the <strong>proximal cheeks</strong>, <strong>central chin</strong>, <strong>nose</strong> and <strong>central forehead</strong>. They get aggravated on <strong>sun exposure</strong>. On examination, <strong>telangiectasia</strong> is seen on the <strong>cheeks</strong>. What is the most probable cause?

A. Polymorphic light eruption
B. SLE
C. Acne vulgaris
D. Rosacea

LLM answer: D. Rosacea
