A <strong>10-year-old boy</strong> has multiple <strong>tan-colored patches</strong> on his skin, and <strong>freckle-like skin changes</strong> in his <strong>armpit area</strong>. The rest of <strong>clinical examination is normal</strong>. Which of the following conditions is also found in patients with this <strong>disorder</strong> as they get older?

A. Bilateral eighth nerve tumors
B. Irregular small pupils
C. Multiple cutaneous and subcutaneous tumors
D. Cataracts

LLM answer: C. Multiple cutaneous and subcutaneous tumors
