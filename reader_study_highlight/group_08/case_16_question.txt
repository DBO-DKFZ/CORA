A <strong>male patient</strong> presents with <strong>patchy loss of hair</strong> on <strong>scalp</strong>, <strong>eyebrows</strong> and <strong>beard</strong>. He also gives a history of <strong>rapid graying of hair</strong> in a few areas. The likely diagnosis is –

A. Alopecia areata
B. Anagen effluvium
C. Telogen effluvium
D. Androgenic alopecia

LLM answer: A. Alopecia areata
