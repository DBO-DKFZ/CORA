A <strong>6-month-old</strong> <strong>infant girl</strong> is brought by her parents to the <strong>emergency room</strong> due to <strong>abnormal jerky movements</strong> of the <strong>upper and lower limbs</strong> for the <strong>past month</strong>. When questioned about her <strong>birth history</strong>, the parents mention that a <strong>prenatal scan</strong> of the <strong>fetal heart</strong> revealed that the patient had a <strong>mass in the left ventricle</strong>, which led to the diagnosis of a <strong>neurocutaneous disorder</strong> in the child. Which of the following findings is a characteristic <strong>cutaneous finding</strong> associated with this young patient’s <strong>disorder</strong>?

A. Ash-leaf spots
B. Cafe-au-lait spots
C. Cavernous hemangioma of the skin
D. Cutaneous neurofibromas
E. Port-wine stain

LLM answer: B. Cafe-au-lait spots
