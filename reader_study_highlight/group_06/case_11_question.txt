A <strong>53-year-old male</strong> who consumes <strong>alcohol</strong> in <strong>heavy doses</strong> for a <strong>long time</strong>, with documented evidence of <strong>vitamin B6 deficiency</strong>, presents with a <strong>pigmented rash on sun-exposed areas</strong>, <strong>bright red tongue</strong>, <strong>diarrhea</strong>, <strong>apathy</strong>, <strong>memory loss</strong>, and <strong>disorientation</strong>. What could be the cause of these features?

A. Niacin deficiency
B. Riboflavin deficiency
C. Folate deficiency
D. Thiamine deficiency

LLM answer: A. Niacin deficiency
