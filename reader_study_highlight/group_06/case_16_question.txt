Since then, his hair has gradually become thinner over the <strong>crown</strong> of his head. He is otherwise healthy and takes no medications. Examination shows <strong>diffuse, nonscarring hair loss</strong> over the <strong>scalp</strong> with a <strong>bitemporal pattern of recession</strong>. B. Clomipramine
C. Griseofulvin
D. Triamcinolone
