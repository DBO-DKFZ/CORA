A <strong>19-year-old</strong> man presents to his <strong>primary care physician</strong> for <strong>evaluation before going off to college</strong>. Specifically, he wants to know how to <strong>stay healthy while living outside his home</strong>. Since <strong>childhood</strong> he has suffered <strong>severe sunburns</strong> even when he goes outside for a <strong>small period of time</strong>. He has also developed many <strong>freckles</strong> and <strong>rough-surfaced growths</strong> starting at the <strong>same age</strong>. Finally, his <strong>eyes are very sensitive</strong> and become <strong>irritated, bloodshot, and painful</strong> after being outside. A defect in a protein with which of the following <strong>functions</strong> is most likely responsible for this patient's <strong>symptoms</strong>?

A. Distinguishing methylated from unmethylated strands
B. Endonucleolytic removal of bases from backbone
C. Recognition of chemically dimerized bases
D. Recognition of mismatched bases
E. Sister chromatid binding and recombination

LLM answer: C. Recognition of chemically dimerized bases
