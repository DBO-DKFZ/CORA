A <strong>newborn infant</strong> is noted to have <strong>numerous, light brown macules</strong> dispersed across her skin. The significance of this feature is due to its strong association with the development of which of the following <strong>tumors</strong>?

A. Basal cell carcinoma
B. Neuroblastoma
C. Neurofibroma
D. Retinoblastoma

LLM answer: C. Neurofibroma
