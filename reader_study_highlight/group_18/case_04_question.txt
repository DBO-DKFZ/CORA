A <strong>middle-aged</strong> man presented with <strong>multiple flaccid bullae</strong> and <strong>oral mucosal lesions</strong>. The most likely finding would be:

A. Granular IgA in reticular dermis
B. Fishnet IgG in epidermis
C. Linear IgG at the dermoepidermal junction
D. Linear IgA in dermal papillae

LLM answer: B. Fishnet IgG in epidermis
