A patient has been diagnosed with <strong>systemic sclerosis</strong>. He has <strong>anti-RNA polymerase III antibodies</strong>. Which of the following is more common in this patient?

A. Reduced risk of scleroderma renal crisis
B. Acute onset of disease
C. Reduced risk of malignancy
D. Increased risk of pulmonary hypertension

LLM answer: B. Acute onset of disease
