A <strong>40-year-old male</strong> had <strong>multiple blisters</strong> over the <strong>trunk and extremities</strong>. <strong>Direct immunofluorescence</strong> studies showed <strong>linear IgG deposits along the basement membrane</strong>. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

A. Pemphigus vulgaris
B. Bullous pemphigoid
C. Pemphigus foliaceus
D. Dermatitis herpetiformis

LLM answer: B. Bullous pemphigoid
