A <strong>19-year-old man</strong> presents to the clinic because he is concerned about a <strong>pigmented skin lesion</strong>. Which of the following characteristics suggests a <strong>dysplastic nevus</strong> (<strong>atypical mole</strong>) rather than a <strong>benign acquired nevus</strong>?

A. uniform tan color
B. located on buttock
C. 4 mm in diameter
D. 20 similar lesions on body

LLM answer: B. located on buttock
