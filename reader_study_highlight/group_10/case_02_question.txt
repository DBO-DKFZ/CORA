A <strong>neonate</strong> has a <strong>large</strong>, <strong>purplish</strong>, <strong>slightly raised lesion</strong> on the <strong>skin of the forehead</strong> near the <strong>temporal area</strong>. As the <strong>child grows</strong>, the <strong>lesion grows</strong> as well and becomes increasingly <strong>prominent</strong>. This lesion may be a component of which of the following <strong>syndromes</strong> or <strong>diseases</strong>?

A. Buerger's disease
B. Milroy's disease
C. Osler-Weber-Rendu disease
D. Sturge-Weber syndrome

LLM answer: D. Sturge-Weber syndrome
