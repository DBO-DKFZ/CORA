A <strong>25-year-old</strong> lady develops <strong>brown macular lesions</strong> over the <strong>bridge of the nose and cheeks</strong> following <strong>exposure to sunlight</strong>. What is the most probable diagnosis?

A. Chloasma
B. Photodermatitis
C. SLE
D. Acne rosacea

LLM answer: A. Chloasma
