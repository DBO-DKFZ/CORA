A <strong>healthy</strong>, <strong>16-year-old girl</strong> is brought in by her mother for a <strong>wellness visit</strong>. During the appointment, the patient’s mother brings up concerns about her daughter’s <strong>acne</strong>. The patient has had <strong>acne for 2 years</strong>. She washes her face <strong>twice a day</strong> with <strong>benzoyl peroxide</strong> and has been on <strong>doxycycline for 2 months</strong> with only <strong>mild improvement</strong>. The patient does not feel that the acne is related to her <strong>menstrual cycles</strong>. The patient’s mother states she does well in school and is the <strong>captain</strong> of the <strong>junior varsity cross-country team</strong>. She is worried that the acne is starting to affect her daughter’s <strong>self-esteem</strong>. The patient states that <strong>prom</strong> is coming up, and she is considering not going because she hates taking pictures. Upon physical exam, there are multiple <strong>open and closed comedones</strong> and scattered, <strong>red nodules</strong> on the patient’s <strong>face</strong> with evidence of <strong>scarring</strong>. The patient’s mother says her neighbor’s son tried <strong>isotretinoin</strong> and wants to know if that may work for her daughter. While talking about the <strong>risk factors for isotretinoin</strong>, you mention that patient will need to be on <strong>2 forms of birth control</strong>. The mother asks, “Is that really necessary? We are a very <strong>religious family</strong> and my daughter knows our household rule about <strong>no sex before marriage</strong>.” Which of the following is the <strong>next step in management</strong>?

A. Ask the mother to leave the room before talking to the patient about her sexual activity
B. Have the patient take a pregnancy test to prove abstinence
C. Prescribe the isotretinoin after giving the patient a handout about birth control methods
D. Prescribe the isotretinoin as the patient does not need additional contraception if she is abstinent
E. Talk to patient and mother about patient’s sexual activity, since parental permission is needed for isotretinoin

LLM answer: A. Ask the mother to leave the room before talking to the patient about her sexual activity
