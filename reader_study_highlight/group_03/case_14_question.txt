A <strong>36-year-old man</strong> is admitted to the <strong>emergency department</strong> with a <strong>painful skin rash</strong> on the <strong>dorsum of his nose</strong>. Physical examination reveals that a <strong>herpetic lesion</strong> is affecting the <strong>dorsum of the nose</strong> and the <strong>eyeball</strong>. Which of the following nerves is most likely to be responsible for transmission of the virus to the <strong>eye</strong>?

A. Nasociliary
B. Supratrochlear
C. Infraorbital
D. Posterior ethmoidal

LLM answer: A. Nasociliary
