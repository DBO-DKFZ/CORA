A <strong>twenty-one-year-old woman</strong> complains that <strong>regular, gentle brushing</strong> of her teeth is <strong>painful</strong> besides causing <strong>profuse bleeding</strong>. <strong>Oral examination</strong> reveals the <strong>loss of epithelium</strong> from the <strong>attached gingiva</strong> of <strong>both arches</strong>. Which of the following <strong>dermatological problems</strong> is this patient most likely to have?

A. Benign mucous membrane pemphigoid
B. Chronic discoid lupus erythematosus
C. Pemphigus
D. Psoriasis

LLM answer: A. Benign mucous membrane pemphigoid
