A <strong>27-year-old</strong> man with <strong>AIDS</strong> develops a <strong>reddish, slightly raised rash</strong> on his <strong>face, neck, and mouth</strong>, consistent in appearance with <strong>Kaposi's sarcoma</strong>. Microscopically, the proliferating cells in this <strong>malignancy</strong> most closely resemble which of the following?

A. Angiosarcoma
B. Carcinosarcoma
C. Lymphoma
D. Malignant fibrous histiocytoma

LLM answer: A. Angiosarcoma
