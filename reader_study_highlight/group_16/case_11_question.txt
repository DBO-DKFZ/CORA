A patient presents with a <strong>single hypopigmented anaesthetic patch</strong> with a <strong>satellite lesion</strong> on the <strong>forearm</strong>. The most likely diagnosis is:

A. Indeterminate leprosy
B. Neuritic leprosy
C. Lepromatous leprosy
D. Tuberculoid leprosy

LLM answer: D. Tuberculoid leprosy
