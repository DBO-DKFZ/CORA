A <strong>72-year-old woman</strong> presented with <strong>skin changes</strong> over her <strong>left breast</strong>. She had a history of <strong>breast cancer</strong> <strong>5 years earlier</strong> and was treated with a <strong>lumpectomy</strong> and <strong>radiation therapy</strong>. What is the diagnosis?

A. Inflammatory breast cancer
B. Mastitis
C. Radiation-induced angiosarcoma
D. Fat necrosis

LLM answer: C. Radiation-induced angiosarcoma
