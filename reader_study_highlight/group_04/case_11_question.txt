A <strong>5-year-old child</strong> predominantly on a <strong>maize-based diet</strong> presents with <strong>recurrent diarrhea</strong>, <strong>dermatitis</strong> with <strong>rashes in the neck area</strong>. What is the most probable diagnosis?

A. Beriberi
B. Acrodermatitis enteropathica
C. Pellagra
D. Tyrosinemia

LLM answer: C. Pellagra
