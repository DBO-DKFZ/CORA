A child with <strong>recurrent mouth ulcers</strong> which usually begin as a <strong>round yellowish elevated spot</strong> surrounded by a <strong>red halo</strong> and heal within <strong>7–10 days</strong>:

A. Gingivitis
B. Aphthous ulcers
C. Malignancy
D. Herpetic ulcer

LLM answer: B. Aphthous ulcers
